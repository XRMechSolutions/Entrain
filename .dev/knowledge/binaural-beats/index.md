# Binaural Beats — Science & Evidence

What the peer-reviewed evidence actually supports about binaural beats and
brainwave entrainment, with honest evidence-grading.

**TL;DR:** The *perceptual mechanism* is solid science. The claim that beats
*entrain the brain into a mental state* is weak and largely unsupported —
binaural beats are the *weakest* of the rhythmic-audio methods. Outcome effects
are modest (g≈0.40–0.45) and rest on small, unblinded, heterogeneous studies;
acute anxiety is the best signal. The famous 40 Hz Alzheimer's research uses
flicker/clicks, *not* binaural beats. Safe for general use; make no medical claims.
