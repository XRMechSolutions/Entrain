# Combining Binaural Beats with Isochronic Tones

## They modulate different things, so they stack

| Method | What it modulates | Where the rhythm lives | Headphones |
|--------|-------------------|------------------------|------------|
| Binaural beat | Frequency — two detuned tones, one per ear | Phantom percept computed in the brainstem (physically absent) | Required |
| Isochronic tone | Amplitude — one tone pulsed on/off | Physically present in the envelope | Not required |

Because one works in the frequency domain and the other in the amplitude domain,
both can be applied to the same sound at once. (Established by definition / signal
structure.)

## How to combine (technique)

1. **Pulse the binaural pair.** Keep the detuned left/right tones and apply an
   on/off amplitude envelope to **both channels with one identical envelope**. The
   result carries the phantom binaural beat *and* a physically-present isochronic
   pulse.
2. **Same rate** — binaural difference and pulse at the same Hz: two mechanisms on
   one target. They can be frequency-matched but not truly phase-locked (the
   binaural beat's phase is neural/arbitrary).
3. **Different rates** — e.g. a 10 Hz binaural difference with a 6 Hz pulse: layers
   two target rhythms. No evidence this confers special benefit; useful for
   experimentation.

**Rule:** gate both ears with the SAME envelope. Independent per-ear gating
destroys the interaural relationship and removes the binaural beat.

## Caveats

- **Hard gating weakens the binaural percept.** The brainstem needs signal
  continuity to lock onto the interaural phase difference; very short on-times give
  the beat too little time to form. Prefer a higher duty cycle and a smooth
  (trapezoidal / raised-cosine) envelope over a harsh square. Smooth edges also
  prevent the clicks a hard on/off produces.
- **The isochronic part is the stronger driver.** Physically-present rhythms drive
  a stronger cortical response than the binaural illusion (see
  `does-entrainment-happen.md`). In a combined stimulus the pulse does most of the
  work; the binaural detuning mainly adds a spatial/phantom quality. Combine for
  the experience, not as a proven multiplier.
- **No direct efficacy evidence** exists for combined protocols; they are even
  less studied than either method alone.
